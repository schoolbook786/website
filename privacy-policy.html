Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

At LiveSK.in, accessible from https://livesk.in/, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. We collect personal details such as name, email address, phone number, and project requirement details when you contact us or place an order for website or app development services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our web & app development services.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our website offerings.
  • Communicate with you regarding project updates, domain/hosting renewals, or support.

3. Hosting & Third-Party Services

We use Blogger, Firebase Firestore, and third-party domain/hosting providers to deliver our services. Your data shared for domain registration or database setup will be handled according to their respective privacy protocols.

We implement strict security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal credentials or business information to outside parties.
